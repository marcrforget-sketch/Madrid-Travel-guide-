7-Day Madrid Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival and La Latina

Settle in, explore Cava Baja and enjoy a first tapas crawl.

Day 2: Royal Madrid

Visit the Royal Palace, Almudena Cathedral and Plaza de Oriente.

Day 3: Historic Centre

See Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor and multicultural Lavapiés.

Day 4: Architecture Day

Walk Gran Vía, visit Templo de Debod and tour the Bernabéu.

Day 5: Cathedrals and Parks

See San Francisco el Grande, Almudena and Retiro Park.

Day 6: Malasaña and Chueca

Explore independent shops, cafés and lively neighbourhood streets.

Day 7: Farewell Madrid

Enjoy one last breakfast before departing from Atocha.
