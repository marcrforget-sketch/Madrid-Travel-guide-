Suggested 7-Day Journey

Day 1 • La Latina

Arrive, explore charming streets, markets, and enjoy a tapas crawl along Cava Baja.

Day 2 • Royal Palace

Visit Palacio Real, Almudena Cathedral, and panoramic viewpoints.

Day 3 • Historic Madrid

Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, Mercado de San Miguel, and Lavapiés.

Day 4 • Architecture Tour

Gran Vía, Templo de Debod, Bernabéu Stadium, and spectacular rooftop views.

Day 5 • Cathedrals & Parks

San Francisco el Grande, Almudena Cathedral, Retiro Park, and Atocha.

Day 6 • Trendy Neighbourhoods

Explore Malasaña, Chueca, Salamanca, shopping, cafés, and nightlife.

Day 7 • Farewell Madrid

Final stroll through La Latina and departure from Atocha Station.
