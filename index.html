7-Day Madrid Itinerary

Day 1 • Arrival & La Latina

Explore Cava Baja and enjoy your first Madrid tapas crawl.

Day 2 • Royal Palace

Visit Palacio Real, Almudena Cathedral and Plaza de Oriente.

Day 3 • Historic Madrid

Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor and Lavapiés.

Day 4 • Architecture Day

Gran Vía, Cibeles, Templo de Debod and Bernabéu Stadium.

Day 5 • Cathedrals & Parks

San Francisco el Grande, Almudena and Retiro Park.

Day 6 • Malasaña & Chueca

Shopping, cafés and Madrid nightlife.

Day 7 • Departure

One last coffee in La Latina before heading to Atocha.
